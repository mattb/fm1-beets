# Beets

Two breaks. Eight slices each. Five independent chances to change the groove.

Beets for the FM-1 ports Matt Biddulph's Norns loop instrument to a shared C engine
that also runs in your browser, with all ten original demo recordings.

- [Play in the browser](emu/)
- [Read the controls](manual/)

Power on, press PLAY, and turn REPEAT or SKIP. The two banks of white keys always
play their own deck. OCT− / OCT+ select which deck the knobs and sample keys edit.

This development port has been compiled and simulated; physical FM-1 installation
and real-time hardware performance have not yet been verified.

Original Beets: MIT, Matt Biddulph. Demo loops: vcvcvc_val.
Platform and adapted template files: MPL-2.0, fm1-base/felucca-bsp contributors.
